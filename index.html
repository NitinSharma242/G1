        Before you continue

        What Gi1 uses, and why

        Here's what each of Gi1's counters asks for, and what it's used for. Your browser or Google still shows its own permission prompt before anything below is actually switched on.

        📷
        Camera / photo library

        Report Issue lets you take or choose a photo of the problem. Your browser asks for camera access itself, only when you tap "Take a photo".

        🤖
        AI photo reading

        That photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude to read it, match it to a category, and draft the complaint text for you to review. This only happens once you upload a photo and tap "Report".

        📍
        Location you type

        The location and state you type in are used only to look up the concerned authority in Gi1's own directory, and to appear in your complaint. Gi1 never reads your device's GPS.

        ✉️
        Gmail account (optional)

        Only if you choose to connect it, so Gi1 can send the complaint straight from your inbox. Google shows its own sign-in and consent screen for this, and Gi1 asks again, separately, right before you connect.

        Denying is fine — you can still browse Gi1, but Report Issue's AI drafting and Gmail sending stay off until you allow this. Revisit anytime from your profile menu → "Data & permissions".

        One more thing

        Connect your Gmail?

        If you continue, Google will ask you to sign in and approve Gi1 for two things:

        ✉️
        Send email as you

        So Gi1 can send your complaint directly from your Gmail account instead of handing it to your mail app.

        👤
        Your Gmail address

        So Gi1 can show which account a complaint was sent from, in the Tracker counter.

        Gi1 never reads your inbox — sending is the only permission it asks Google for. You can disconnect anytime from Report Issue.
